What it is

BSTM (Botswana Smart Technology Makers) is an ecosystem of connected digital projects — trading tools, marketplaces, a digital currency, transport software, and creative work — built to solve practical problems through technology, finance, and design.

Why it exists

BSTM exists to build practical technology that creates opportunities, supports innovation, and shows what can be built from Botswana through consistent, connected work.
